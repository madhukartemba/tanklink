## Changes

- Auto-fill on the home screen: a button under the tank turns it on or off and shows what it is doing, and tank chips mark the auto-fill tank.
- Auto-fill is for one tank per pump. Moving it to another tank asks first.
- The Auto-fill screen shows a warning and lets you pick which tank the valve or lever is set to. When another tank is selected, it offers to select the auto-fill tank.

Goes with pump 0.32.0 and tank 0.7.1 (unchanged). **Update the pump and the app together.** Auto-fill is off after the update: turn it on again for your tank.
