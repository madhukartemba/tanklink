## Changes

- Auto-fill is for one tank per pump. Turning it on for a tank turns it off for the others.
- Auto-fill only runs while its tank is selected, so the valve or lever has to point at it. With another tank selected it waits.
- Smoother press-and-hold light: the glow spreads and shrinks evenly instead of in steps.

Goes with app 1.19.0 and tank 0.7.1 (unchanged). **Update the pump and the app together.** Auto-fill is off after the update: turn it on again for your tank.
