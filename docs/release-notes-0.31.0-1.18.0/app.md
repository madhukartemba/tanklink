## Changes

- New Auto-fill screen: which tanks fill themselves, allowed times with days, the wait between fills, and what auto-fill is doing right now, with Fill now and Resume.
- New Buzzer screen: a switch and a sound for each of 12 events, with a preview on the pump, and quiet hours.
- Fill history shows "Allowed time ended" when auto-fill stops at the end of its time.

Goes with pump 0.31.0 and tank 0.7.1 (unchanged). **Update the pump and the app together.** Buzzer settings go back to their defaults once.
