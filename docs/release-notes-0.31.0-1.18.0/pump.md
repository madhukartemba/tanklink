## Changes

- Auto-fill has allowed times: up to 4 time windows with days of the week, including ones past midnight. Outside them a low tank waits for the next window. It can also stop a fill when its time ends and carry on in the next window.
- Choose how long auto-fill waits after a stop before it starts again (5 minutes to 3 hours).
- Turning auto-fill on fills a tank that is already low, and a paused auto-fill can be resumed from the app.
- The pump tells the app what auto-fill is doing: watching, waiting for an allowed time, waiting between fills, paused, or filling.
- Buzzer: pick which of 12 events make a sound (button, start refused, started or stopped from the app, schedule, auto-fill, tank full, auto-off, alerts, pairing, phone connects, power on) and one of 9 sounds for each.
- Buzzer quiet hours, with the button still sounding if you want.
- Sounds no longer hold up the pump while they play.

Goes with app 1.18.0 and tank 0.7.1 (unchanged). **Update the pump and the app together**: older apps can't read this pump's settings. Buzzer settings go back to their defaults once; set them again in Buzzer. Other settings are kept.
