## Changes

- The tank's button always works now, including the long press to pair. Before, pressing it while the tank was blinking an error (such as "not paired") could leave it asleep and unresponsive until the reset button was pressed.
- A press that lands just as the tank goes to sleep still counts: letting go wakes it, and a long hold still starts pairing.
- Error blinks stop as soon as you press the button.
- A stuck or bouncing button can no longer keep the tank awake or unpair it by itself.
- The soft "sent" pulse is back on every update the pump answers, including the 5-minute heartbeat.

Goes with pump 0.28.1 and app 1.16.0 (both unchanged). No layout change: nothing to pair or set again. A tank stuck asleep on 0.5.1 or older needs its reset button pressed once before it can be updated.
