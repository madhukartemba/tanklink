## Changes

- Holding the pump button: at 1 second it beeps, and the light turns green if letting go will start the pump, or red with a double beep if it can't (tank full, not paired, an update running).
- The button light starts and grows smoothly, even on a quick tap, and fades straight into the running light when the pump starts.
- "Fill when low" now works on a tank with only its full float: the pump tops the tank up when the water drops below the float.

Goes with app 1.17.0 and tank 0.7.1 (unchanged). Update the pump and the app together to use "Fill when low" with one float. Nothing to pair or set again.
