TankLink
Local first. No cloud control.

Your water tank,
under control.

A pump controller that talks straight to your tank sensor and your phone. No subscription, no account.

Download for Android

Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time.

What you get

Stops when full

The pump stops by itself when the tank reports full, and starts are blocked while it is.

Up to 8 tanks

Pair several sensors and pick which tank the pump fills.

Schedules and auto-off

Set fill times and a safety limit for each tank.

Alerts that find you

Tank full, power cut, tank offline and low battery show on your phone even with the app closed.

Water and power use

See litres, energy and cost worked out from pump run time.

Updates itself

On home Wi-Fi the pump checks for new firmware and installs it when idle.
